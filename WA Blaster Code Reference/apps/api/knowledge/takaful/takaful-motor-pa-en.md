# Takaful myMotor — Motor PA Plus & Bike PA Plus (Add-on Benefits)

> **Plan:** Takaful &middot; **Product:** Add-on benefits for Takaful myMotor — Motor PA Plus (Private Car) and Bike PA Plus (Motorcycle) &middot; **Operator:** Syarikat Takaful Malaysia Am Berhad [201701032316 (1246486-D)].
> **Source:** `MPA_takaful.pdf`, 2 pages.

## Motor PA Plus — Private Car Add-on Benefits
Personal Accident add-on for Takaful myMotor (Private Car). The Personal Accident benefit is per person.

| Benefit | Plan 1 | Plan 2 | Plan 3 | Plan 4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Contribution | RM20 | RM30 | RM40 | RM85 |
| Complimentary PA (per person) | RM15,000 | RM15,000 | RM15,000 | RM15,000 |
| Add-On PA (per person) | RM20,000 | RM30,000 | RM40,000 | RM85,000 |
| **Total PA benefit (per person)** | **RM35,000** | **RM45,000** | **RM55,000** | **RM100,000** |
| Maximum mileage (Towing Tele Bantuan) | 100 km | 200 km | 300 km | Unlimited (up to 1,000 km for East Malaysia) |
| Breakdown towing | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Accident towing | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

- **Example:** the maximum PA benefit payable for a 7-seater vehicle is RM700,000 (Plan 4: RM100,000 per person × 7).
- These add-on benefits can be included in the following motor cover: (1) Comprehensive Private Car Takaful; (2) Third Party, Fire & Theft Private Car Takaful.
- Towing assistance is provided via the Tele Bantuan service.

## Bike PA Plus — Motorcycle Add-on Benefits
Personal Accident add-on for Takaful myMotor (Motorcycle). Includes one-time free towing up to 30 km or roadside assistance for all Comprehensive Motorcycle Takaful certificate holders.

| Benefit | Plan 1 | Plan 2 | Plan 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Contribution | RM15 | RM22 | RM25 |
| Accidental Death & Permanent Disablement — Authorised Rider | RM3,000 | RM5,000 | RM3,000 |
| Badal Haji — Authorised Rider | RM2,000 | RM2,500 | RM2,000 |
| Qurban — Authorised Rider | RM500 | RM500 | RM500 |
| Accidental Death & Permanent Disablement — Pillion Passenger | – | – | RM3,000 |
| Badal Haji — Pillion Passenger | – | – | RM2,000 |
| Qurban — Pillion Passenger | – | – | RM500 |
| **Total** | **RM5,500** | **RM8,000** | **RM11,000** |

- These add-on benefits can be included in the following motor cover: (1) Comprehensive Motorcycle Takaful; (2) Third Party Motorcycle Takaful.
- The benefits are only provided one (1) time during the period of takaful for both rider & pillion passenger. There is no age limit applicable to the pillion passenger for PA cover.
- The total benefit amount payable for Non-Muslims will be the same as the total benefit amount received by Muslims as stated in the schedule.
