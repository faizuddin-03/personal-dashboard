2.0 REQUIREMENT

2.1 High-Level Requirement Overview

#      Item                          Description

2.1.1  Enhancement Overview          Split JomCheck charge on invoice and e-Invoice into 2 line items:
                                     JomCheck service charge and eAuto service charge.

2.1.2  Key Value(s) Expected         Invoice and e-Invoice show actual fee breakdown, while total amount
                                     paid by UCD stays same.

2.1.3  Additional Note(s)            Applies only to JomCheck transactions approved after deployment of
                                     this Change Request.

2.2.2 Existing Issue & Expected Behaviour

#  Item                        Description

1  Existing Issue / Behaviour  Invoice shows only 1 line item, JomCheck service charge, at RM20.00 plus 8% SST,
                               which is RM21.60.

                               1. Unit Price: RM20.00
                               2. Tax Amount: RM1.60
                               3. Total Price (including tax): RM21.60

                               Amount and fees shown do not reflect actual fee breakdown.

2  Expected New Handling /     JomCheck charge is split into 2 line items, each charged 8% SST:
                                   1. JomCheck service charge: RM18.00 + SST RM1.44 = RM19.44.
   Behaviour                       2. eAuto service charge: RM2.00 + SST RM0.16 = RM2.16.

                               Total charge remains RM21.60 including SST.
                               Same breakdown applies to invoice and e-Invoice.
                               Applies only to JomCheck transactions approved after deployment.
