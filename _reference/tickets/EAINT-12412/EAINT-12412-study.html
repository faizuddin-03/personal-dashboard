08Important for QA

  1. The invoice and the reports are meant to differ. The invoice shows an eAuto line of RM2.00; the revenue, SST and e-Invoice reports keep eAuto at RM6. This is by design (Option A), not a defect.
  2. The cut-off test needs old data. Prepare a transaction approved before deployment, and one created before but approved after, ahead of the deploy to the test environment.
  3. Compare UCD and BO invoices for the same transaction, line by line (REQ-005).
  4. Regression on shared e-Invoice code. The dev says eSTM, insurance and APT share e-Invoice code but do not change. Service Hub already sends multi-line e-Invoices. Both statements are unverified.
  5. Unchanged per the dev: JomCheck cancellations, and invoices from before July 2025, which keep their old layout.
  6. Existing automation in scripts/eauto-jomcheck already downloads both PDFs (TS04) but does not read their amounts. A PDF text check covers REQ-002 to REQ-005.
  7. eSim limit: the JomCheck API allows 20 reports per day.
