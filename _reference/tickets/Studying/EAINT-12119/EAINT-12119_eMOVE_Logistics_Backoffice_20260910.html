Hub Management

Maintain the logistics hub records. An address is checked against Google Maps, then confirmed by Operations before the hub can be used. Distance is measured between hub coordinates, so the pin must sit on the right place. Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan hubs may be recorded here; the restriction applies on the UCD portal only.

Google Match — what each value means

How precisely Google placed the address. Distance is measured between hub coordinates, so an imprecise pin gives the wrong distance, the wrong tier and the wrong price.

ValueWhat it meansTypical errorCan the hub be used
Exact addressMatched the building at that street numberWithin metresYes
Street levelMatched the street but not the exact premises100 to 200 metresYes
NeighbourhoodMatched only an estate or area. Google returns the centre point of that areaUp to about 2 kmNo, Operations must confirm or set the pin
Different placeMatched a location that is not the one recorded10 km or moreNo, the address is likely wrong
Set by OperationsCoordinates entered by hand after review, replacing what Google returnedNot applicableYes
