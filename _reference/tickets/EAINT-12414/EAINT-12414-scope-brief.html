EAINT-12414 · eAuto Onboarding · UCD Pre-Application Form

Form resets on reload, and a failed card payment fails silently

Status To Do Priority Medium Environment staging/obs Dev Mong Guo Hao Fix MR !331 (review)

What's going wrong

Two separate defects, both on the public Pre-Application Form's payment step — one loses the applicant's progress, the other hides a failure they need to know about.

1

Reloading the form drops you back to Step 1

An applicant fills in Step 1, moves on to Step 2, then refreshes or reopens the page — expecting to continue where they left off. Instead the form resets to the very beginning, and their progress, including their Type of Business selection, is gone.

Root cause

A list the page needs while loading is only built after loading has already started. The page errors partway through and falls back to Step 1.

2

A failed card payment gives no warning

Once issue 1 is fixed, this one surfaces: if a Credit or Debit Card payment fails and the gateway sends the browser back to the same tab (rather than the payment popup), the form reloads with no message. The agreement checkbox is unticked and the payment options vanish — the applicant has no idea the payment failed or how to retry.

Expected message

Unsuccessful Payment — Something went wrong and your payment didn't go through. Please choose a payment method and retry.

No money is deducted on a failed payment — there's no refund exposure — but both bugs risk applicants abandoning the form or contacting support over something that isn't actually broken on their end.

Test scope

  1. Reload restores Step 2, Type of Business intact defect Fill to Step 2, refresh, confirm the form resumes at Step 2 with the Type of Business value correctly restored. Pre-Application only.
  2. Failed card payment, same-tab return defect Force a decline via the Fiuu sandbox response picker with the gateway returning in-tab. Confirm the banner appears, the applicant lands on Step 2, T&C stays ticked, payment methods stay visible.
  3. Failed card payment, popup return regression Same forced decline via the popup path — already correct today, confirm it still is.
  4. Successful card payment regression Confirm the happy path for Credit/Debit Card is unchanged by the fix.
  5. Successful FPX (Personal) payment regression Confirm unchanged. FPX B2B is excluded — not deployed yet.
  6. No repeat banner after a second reload defect After seeing the failure message once, reload again — the banner must not reappear on its own.
  7. Cross-browser spot check regression Chrome and Firefox only.
  8. Regression — Full Pre-Application & Application Happy Flow regression One complete walkthrough end to end — gate → pre-application → BO approval → application form → BO assign/approve → registration documents → BO verify → registration fee → record. Default values, one pass, no negative branches. Purpose is narrow: confirm the fix hasn't broken the main path downstream.
