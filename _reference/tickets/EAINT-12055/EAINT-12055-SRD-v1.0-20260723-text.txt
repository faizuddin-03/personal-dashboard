2.0  REQUIREMENT 
2.1  High-Level Requirement Overview 
# 
Item 
Description 
2.1.1 
 
Enhancement Overview 
 
Exclude a payment and its matched approved transaction from the Reconciliation 
Listing, even when the two fall on different dates. 
 
2.1.2 
 
Key Value(s) Expected 
 
The listing shows only unmatched transactions, so matched pairs no longer 
appear as duplicate lines. 
 

===== PAGE 6 =====
2.1.1  Assumptions 
# 
Assumption 
1 
 
A payment and an approved transaction are considered a matched pair when they share the same Payment Ref No. 
 
2 
 
For a matched pair, the Approved Amount in eAuto and the Payment Amount in RHB offset each other (one value 
against the other for the same reference). 
 
3 
 
The existing reconciliation matching logic remains unchanged apart from broadening the date comparison to allow 
different dates and cross-month matching.

2.2.2  Existing Issue & Expected Behaviour 
# 
Item 
Description 
1 
 
Existing Issue / Behaviour 
 
1. When a transaction is approved on a different date from the payment date into 
the RHB account, it appears as two separate lines in the Recon list. 
2. Each of the two lines carries a different “Refund Required” value (e.g. one 
“Yes” and one “No”), even though the payment is already matched with the 
approved transaction. 
 
2 
 
Expected New Handling / 
Behaviour 
 
1. The listing should not show a matched payment and approved transaction 
even when they occur on different dates. 
2. The date matching should be broadened to also match across different 
months (e.g. payment on 30 July 2026 and approved transaction on 1 August 
2026). 
3. When the two transactions are matched, both are aborted (removed) from the 
listing.

# 
Item 
Description 
Acceptance Criteria 
1 
 
Exclude matched payments 
 
1. Once a payment is matched with an 
approved transaction, it will no longer be 
displayed in the listing.  
2. Only unmatched payments or those 
requiring further attention will remain 
visible. 
 
A payment that has been matched 
with an approved transaction no 
longer appears in the listing, while 
unmatched payments and those 
requiring further attention remain 
visible. 
 
2 
 
Match transactions across 
months 
 
1. The system shall match a payment with an 
approved transaction even if the payment 
date and transaction approval date fall in 
different calendar months. 
2.  This ensures that valid matches across 
month-end periods are not missed. 
 
Example: A payment made on 30 July shall be 
matched with a transaction approved on 1 
August. 
 
A payment dated 30 July is matched 
with a transaction approved on 1 
August, confirming that matches are 
made even when the dates fall in 
different calendar months.
