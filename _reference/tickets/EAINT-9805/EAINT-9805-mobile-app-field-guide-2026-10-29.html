"Manual TS" in plain words

  1. A test scenario a person carries out by hand, step by step, on a real phone.
  2. It covers what a script cannot do: insert a MyKad, place a thumb, switch Bluetooth off, walk out of range, take a call.
  3. Each row has: what to do, what should happen, which device, which app build, Pass / Fail, remarks.

What the Iris tab must hold

  1. Rows: Iris scenarios built from the 7229 Dermalog sheets. Mapping is in section 07, outline in section 08.
  2. Kept from 7229: MyKad, thumbprint, two-card, button, performance scenarios.
  3. Changed: "unplug the cable" becomes Bluetooth off, out of range, reader powered off.
  4. Added (app only): Bluetooth permission, pairing, unpair, call / Home / lock / kill during a scan, tablet rotation.
  5. Dropped: web-only rows such as browsers, installer download, HMAC (F12), one-tab rule.

One manual run, start to end

  1. Install the UAT build. Note app version and build no.
  2. Mirror the phone to the laptop and start recording.
  3. Do the steps in the TS row by hand.
  4. Compare with the expected result. Mark Pass or Fail.
  5. Fill device, OS, build no. and remarks.
  6. Check the record on web (UCD, BO) where the TS says so.
  7. On Fail: raise a QA-Issue with device model and build no.
Ask at the sync-up: Which devices are mine for the manual run? Section 09 lists the other questions.
03

Mobile testing basics

Web testing checks one page in one browser. Mobile testing checks the same flow across many devices, OS versions, permissions and life events of the app.

What changes compared with web

AreaWeb (what you know)Mobile app (what is new)
InstallOpen a staging URL.Install a UAT build from Play Store (tester list). Update by hand on every new build.
VersionWhatever is deployed.Every bug and retest states app version + build no., phone model, OS.
CoverageChrome, maybe Edge.Android 13–17, iOS 18–27, tablet, iPad. Screen sizes differ.
HardwareDermalog via a local WebSocket service.Dermalog via USB (OTG); Iris via Bluetooth. App asks for USB / Bluetooth permission.
Life cycleTab open or closed.App goes to background, is locked, killed, rotated, interrupted by a call.
LoginUsername + password.Also fingerprint, Face ID, Touch ID.
EvidenceBrowser screenshots.Mirror the phone to the laptop and screen-record (Phone Link, Dr. Fone Air).
Data checkSame portal.App writes to the same database. Confirm on web: UCD and BO listing / details, BO JPJ XML Log.

Words you will meet

Interruption test
Something outside the app breaks in mid-flow: call, notification, Home, lock, network loss. Check the app recovers correctly.
Home button
Sends the app to the background. App stays alive.
Overview button
The square / recent-apps button. Shows open apps to switch between.
Kill the app
Swipe the app away in Overview. Process ends. Reopen starts fresh.
Toast
Small message that pops up at the bottom and fades, e.g. No internet connection.
Edge-to-edge
Android 15+ draws the app under the status bar and navigation bar. Bug sign: buttons or text hidden behind those bars.
Portrait / landscape
Phone upright / sideways. Phase 2 phones stay portrait; Phase 6 tablets must work both ways.
OTG
USB On-The-Go. Lets a phone power and talk to a USB device such as Dermalog. Needs a USB-C adapter.
Pair (Bluetooth)
One-time link between phone and Iris reader. Unpair removes it.
Runtime licence
Dermalog licence needed on the phone. Phase 2 testers without it marked thumbprint rows N/A.
Rooted device
Android phone with admin access unlocked. EAINT-12570 added handling for it (Done).
Build no.
Number that changes on every app build. Same version can have many builds.

What each depth means

  1. Full manual: Iris and Dermalog full testing incl. negative; face and fingerprint login; interruption testing.
  2. Short check (manual): 5 scanner steps per reader (connect, transaction, reconnect, lock/idle, unplug or unpair).
  3. "Can use" (tablet): portrait + landscape on every screen; rotate mid-flow and check data stays.
  4. Edge-to-edge (Android 16/17): nothing hidden under status or navigation bar.

07

Dermalog vs Iris

Dermalog

  1. MyKad + thumbprint reader. Used on web today.
  2. App link: USB cable via OTG. App asks USB permission.
  3. Android 8–15 only. Not iOS, not iPad, not officially Android 16+.
  4. Needs a runtime licence on the phone.

Iris (new)

  1. Teams calls it an iris card reader Kasheng Liew, 16 Oct 2026. It is a card reader brand, not an eye scanner. confirm model
  2. App link: Bluetooth. App asks Bluetooth permission; reader must be paired.
  3. Android 13+ and all iOS / iPad. Not Android 8–12.
  4. Dev status 16 Oct: still some negative scenarios need to test more.

How the EAINT-7229 Dermalog TS maps to Iris

7229 had 85 TS on web. Some carry over almost as-is; some change because the link is Bluetooth; some are web-only.

7229 sheetTSIris treatment
Dermalog TS (unplug / reconnect at 5 points)13Rewrite. "Unplug" becomes 3 Bluetooth events: Bluetooth off, out of range, reader powered off. Same 5 points: before read, during read, after read, during thumbprint, after thumbprint.
MyKad TS8Carry over. Valid, no card, invalid, retry, multiple fails, remove during / after read, non-MyKad card.
Thumbprint TS9Carry over. Right / left thumb, wrong thumb, retries, card removed around the thumbprint. "Refresh page" becomes "leave the step and come back".
2 MyKads TS12Adapt. Two Iris readers in range; Iris + Dermalog both connected (Android 13–15); one reader paired to two phones.
Functional TS ([Cancel], [Reconnect])8Carry over. Button names to confirm in the app.
Auto Scan TS6Ask first. Only if the app has Auto Scan IC.
Connectivity & Security21Split. Network loss rows carry over. HMAC (F12), one-tab rule, applet vs WebSocket are web-only.
Performance TS4+4Carry over. Connect time, MyKad read time, thumbprint time, next-step time; 5 readings and average.
Browsers, Installer download, QA Laptop Specs—Drop. Replaced by the device matrix.
New, app only—Bluetooth permission allow / deny, pairing, unpair, app interruptions during a scan (call, Home, lock, kill), tablet rotation mid-scan, unsupported OS (Android 8–12).

Final web Dermalog messages (for reference, not the Iris expected result)

  1. No device: Please plug in Dermalog Biometric Device and click [Reconnect]
  2. No card: No card is detected. Please insert IC and click [Scan IC] to try again.
  3. No chip: Please ensure the IC is placed properly or the chip surface is cleaned
  4. Read broken: Unable to read photo. Please try again.
  5. Wrong thumb: Verification failed. Please click on [Scan IC] to try again
  6. Good thumb: Verification Passed and Verification Completed

Sections and candidate scenarios

  1. Pairing & permission (new): first pair + allow Bluetooth; deny Bluetooth permission; Bluetooth off before opening the thumbprint step; reader off / not paired; unpair then open the step.
  2. Connection loss (from 7229 Dermalog TS): Bluetooth off / out of range / reader off × before read, during read, after read, during thumbprint, after thumbprint.
  3. Reconnect: connection back at the same 5 points; rapid off / on.
  4. MyKad (from MyKad TS): 8 scenarios.
  5. Thumbprint (from Thumbprint TS): 9 scenarios.
  6. Two readers / two cards: two Iris in range; Iris + Dermalog on one Android 13–15 phone; reader paired to two phones.
  7. Buttons (from Functional TS): [Cancel] at 5 points; rapid [Cancel] / [Reconnect] / [Scan IC].
  8. App interruptions during a scan (from Phase 2): call, notification, Home, Overview, lock, kill, network loss, idle.
  9. Platform: Android 13, 15, 16, 17; iOS; iPad; tablet rotation mid-scan; Android 8–12 message.
  10. Performance: 4 timings, 5 readings each.
Entry points: every app step that needs a thumbprint. On web these are STMS Step 1 Seller and Step 3 Buyer, eSTM Step 1 Buyer and Step 3 Seller, plus UCD to UCD, BMK, eSerahan and Insurance permission pages. Confirm which exist in the Phase 6 app.

Day-one checklist

  1. Send your Google Play email to be added to the UAT tester list (Phase 2: Desmond; confirm the owner now).
  2. Install the UAT app. Note the version and build no. from the login page.
  3. Set up mirroring + recording: Phone Link (Windows) or Dr. Fone Air.
  4. Collect hardware: Iris reader, Dermalog + OTG adapter, valid MyKad A and B, an invalid MyKad, a chip card that is not MyKad, a card without chip.
  5. Run one STMS happy path on web staging first. Then the same in the app. Compare listing and details on web.
  6. Run the 5-step Iris and Dermalog short checks from the device sheet to learn the readers.
  7. Take the questions in section 09 to the sync-up.
  8. Draft the Iris tab from section 08 once expected messages are known.
Raising bugs: Phase 2 rule: Include your Android device model and APP version & build number in your ticket. Retests also need the build no.
