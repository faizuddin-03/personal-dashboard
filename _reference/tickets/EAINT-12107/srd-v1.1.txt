2.0 REQUIREMENT
2.1 High-Level Requirement Overview

#      Item                          Description

2.1.1  Enhancement Overview          The existing Secarang Fiuu payment gateway shall be
                                     enhanced to enable 2 additional payment channels, namely
2.1.2  Key Value(s) Expected         ShopeePay and GrabPay.
2.1.3  Additional Note(s)
                                     Each channel offers a Full Amount option and a Buy Now Pay
                                     Later (BNPL) monthly installment option.

                                     Customers are given more payment channels to choose from
                                     when paying for their insurance in Secarang, including the
                                     option to pay by monthly installment.

                                     SPayLater under ShopeePay carries an activation date of 26
                                     May 2026 and PayLater by Grab under GrabPay carries an
                                     activation date of 21 June 2026.

                                     Both channels shall be released together.

                                     Page 5 of 22
2.1.1 Assumptions

#  Assumption

1  Fiuu has completed the merchant-side activation for both ShopeePay and GrabPay before deployment.

2  The existing payment channels in Secarang remain unchanged.

3  The monthly installment tenure and terms under SPayLater and PayLater by Grab are managed by

   Shopee and Grab respectively, and are not verified in Secarang.
