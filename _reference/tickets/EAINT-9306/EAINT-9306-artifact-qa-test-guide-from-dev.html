EAINT-9306 — eDereg Pre-Checking required before Deregistration

What changed and how to exercise it. Every behaviour below was read from the branch code. Repo eauto_v2, branch dev/feat/EAINT-9306.

The change in one sentence. A Deregistration can no longer be created unless the vehicle already has an Approved + Paid + JPJ-Approved eDereg Pre-Checking (DPC) whose JPJ approval is within the last 6 months. The AATF data-entry page now checks this the moment you leave the vehicle-number field, and if the check fails it creates (or resumes) a precheck and opens the precheck payment dialog inline — without leaving the DEREG form.

1. Where to look

ScreenWhat is new
AATF → Dereg → data entry (vehicle page) The gate itself: the check on leaving the vehicle field, the inline result message, and the precheck payment dialog
AATF home, Dereg landing, Dereg transaction view The compliance announcement banner (same wording in all three)
AATF Dereg enquiry → view New "eDereg Pre-Checking:" row showing Yes or -, linking to the precheck listing. By design this shows Yes if the vehicle has any precheck record at all — successful or failed, any age — so it is not the same test as the gate in step 2
Back office Dereg enquiry → view The same new row, for operations staff
Back office eDereg Pre-Checking listing Now accepts a vehicle number and auto-searches when arrived at from the link above

2. The rule being enforced

A precheck only satisfies the gate when all four hold. Anything else — unpaid, JPJ-rejected, cancelled, expired, or older than six months — must be treated as "no precheck".

  • Transaction status is Approved
  • Payment status is Paid
  • JPJ pre-check enquiry status is Approved
  • The JPJ approval timestamp is within the last 6 months

Same vehicle number and same AATF company — another company's precheck never counts.

Only one record has to satisfy all four. A later failed or cancelled attempt does not cancel out an earlier approved one — if any precheck for that vehicle was approved within the last 6 months, the vehicle can proceed.

3. Test scenarios

  1. Vehicle with a valid precheck → allowed. Leave the vehicle-number field. A green message appears: "This vehicle is allowed to create eDereg transaction". Continue and save the DEREG as normal — it must go through.
  2. Vehicle with no precheck at all → blocked, then offered one. A red message appears: "Approved eDereg Pre-Checking within 6 months is required to proceed", a new precheck is created, and the precheck payment dialog opens on top of the DEREG form. Complete the payment, let the JPJ enquiry come back Approved, then confirm the DEREG can now be saved.
  3. Precheck exists but is not valid → blocked. Try each of: unpaid, JPJ-rejected, Cancelled, Expired, and one approved more than six months ago. All five must be refused with the red message. This is the core of the ticket — worth doing all five rather than one.
  4. A later failure does not undo an earlier approval. On a vehicle with an approved precheck from (say) 1 January and a failed one from 5 January, both within 6 months, the DEREG must still be allowed. The most recent record is the failure, so this confirms the rule looks at any qualifying record rather than just the latest.
  5. Abandoned precheck with no payment → reused, not duplicated. Trigger the gate, let the dialog open, Cancel it, then leave the vehicle field again. You must get the same precheck back — the listing must not grow a second row each time.
  6. Precheck with a failed payment → resumed in retry mode, with its history. Fail a payment, cancel out, then leave the vehicle field again (or reload and redo it). The dialog must reopen on the same precheck in retry mode and show the earlier failed attempts under Payment History. Previously each attempt created a fresh row and the history disappeared — that is what this fixes, so it is the highest-value retest.
  7. Long payment history stays usable. On a precheck with several failed attempts, confirm the history area scrolls inside the dialog and the Cancel / Next buttons stay on screen. Check at 1366×768 or smaller, not just a large monitor.
  8. Another company's precheck does not count. With a vehicle that has a valid precheck under company A, log in as company B and confirm B is still blocked.
  9. The JPJ receipt email is populated. Create a precheck from the DEREG form and confirm the resulting DPC JPJ receipt carries the AATF's precheck email. It was missing on this path until a late fix, so it needs a specific check. If the email is blank, first confirm the AATF company actually has a precheck email configured before raising it.
  10. The enquiry indicator. Open a DEREG transaction in both the AATF and the back-office enquiry views and confirm the "eDereg Pre-Checking" row appears and the link reaches the precheck listing for that vehicle.
  11. The banner. Present and readable on the AATF home page, the Dereg landing page and the Dereg transaction view.

4. What to include when raising a defect

  1. Vehicle number, AATF company, and the precheck reference number
  2. The state of the precheck when you started — status, payment status, JPJ status
  3. Which of the scenarios above you were on, and the exact on-screen message
  4. A screenshot including the dialog, if one was open
  5. Rough timestamp, so the JPJ and payment logs can be lined up

Point 2 matters more than usual here: nearly all of this feature's behaviour depends on the precheck's existing state, so "it blocked me" is only actionable once we know what the vehicle already had on file.
