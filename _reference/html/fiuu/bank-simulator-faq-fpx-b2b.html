What is it?

Simulates FPX B2B (business-to-business) payments for corporate banking: ABB (Affin Bank), ABBM (Affin Bank Multi-Bank), and HLB (Hong Leong Bank).

Test Credentials

No login required. FPX B2B uses a real maker/checker approval flow (two separate decisions by two different people, mirroring how corporate bank accounts actually work) - the simulator reproduces both steps.

How to Test

  1. Initiate an FPX B2B payment from the merchant side.
  2. You will be redirected to the simulator payment page.
  3. Click Authorize (the maker's decision) or Reject.
If you click Authorize: the browser redirects back to the merchant return page showing status=22 (pending). This does not finalize the payment - a separate checker still needs to Approve or Reject it.
  1. Open Pending Checker Decisions on the simulator's main page (or go to /fpx-b2b/pending directly).
  2. Click Approve or Reject for that order - this is the checker's decision, and it happens independently of the customer's browser session (a real checker logs in separately, possibly much later).
