# Flow 3 — When an entry is queried

**Who:** Everyone
**When:** Whenever your lead sends something back. There's nothing to do here until they do.

Your lead can do three things to an entry you logged: send it back with a question, cut the hours, or move it between billable and non-billable. This flow explains what each of those looks like from your side.

---

## Step 1 — Read the query and reply on the entry

When your lead queries an entry, it gets a **Queried** chip on your day, and their reason appears just underneath it with their name on it. The badge in the sidebar counts how many queries you haven't answered yet.

To reply, click **Add to the thread**. Your reply and their response stay attached to that entry, so if anyone reads it months later, they see the question and the answer together instead of having to dig around for the context.

A queried day stays editable **for that one entry**, even after 11:00 PM. The rest of the day is still locked as normal.

**Things worth knowing:**

- **Answering doesn't clear the query.** It moves the entry into your lead's queue — the list they actually work through — and they close it off from there.
- **If your lead queries the whole week rather than one entry, the reason is only stated once.** It appears at the top of your week instead of being copied onto every single entry.

---

## Step 2 — Hours cut, and work reclassified

There are two other things your lead can do without asking you first.

**They can assess the hours lower than you claimed.** If they do, both figures stay on the record — your original claim is shown struck through, with their assessed figure next to it. Nothing gets quietly overwritten, so you can always see what you logged and what was counted.

**They can move an entry between billable and non-billable.** They'll do this if they think you booked it to the wrong side. Again, you can see that it happened and what changed.

**Things worth knowing:**

- **A reclassification is a correction, not an accusation.** The borderline cases are borderline for everybody, and getting one wrong isn't held against you.
- **If your lead reopens the day or week instead, it comes back to you untouched,** and any assessed duration is cleared along with it.
