## Generic motor-insurance basics (14 questions)

- **Q:** hi, what types of car insurance got in malaysia?
  - **Expected:** Three main types: Third Party (the legal minimum), Third Party Fire & Theft, and Comprehensive (the widest cover).
- **Q:** does comprehensive cover fire and theft for my own car?
  - **Expected:** Yes — a comprehensive policy covers accidental loss or damage to your own vehicle including collision, overturning, fire and theft, plus legal liability to third parties.
- **Q:** my car kena flood damage, basic comprehensive cover or not?
  - **Expected:** No — flood, storm and landslide damage is not included in a basic comprehensive policy; you need to add Special Perils cover (the add-on premium is usually a small percentage of your sum insured).
- **Q:** i drive grab part time, my normal car insurance can cover ah?
  - **Expected:** No — a standard private-car policy does not cover e-hailing and a claim may be rejected; you need to add the e-hailing extension to your comprehensive policy.
- **Q:** if my friend drives my car and he's not named in the policy, how much excess?
  - **Expected:** A compulsory excess of RM400 applies when the car is driven by someone not named in the policy (also for drivers under 21, P-licence holders, or full licence held less than two years).
- **Q:** what is my NCD after 3 years no claim?
  - **Expected:** 38.33% after three claim-free years (the scale is 25% after one year, 30% after two, 38.33% after three, 45% after four, 55% from five years onward).
- **Q:** if i make a claim will i lose my NCD?
  - **Expected:** An at-fault own-damage claim resets your NCD to 0% at the next renewal.
- **Q:** got accident just now, how fast must i make police report?
  - **Expected:** Lodge a police report within 24 hours of the accident (and photograph the damage and the scene).
- **Q:** what documents do i need to renew my insurance?
  - **Expected:** Your IC, your vehicle registration card (geran), and your current or previous policy details so your NCD can be confirmed.
- **Q:** my insurance lapsed few months ago, need inspection before insure again?
  - **Expected:** Possibly — if a policy lapses, the car may need a PUSPAKOM inspection before it can be insured again.
- **Q:** can i renew road tax online?
  - **Expected:** Yes — once insured, road tax can be renewed online via MyEG or JPJ, or over the counter at the post office (an active motor insurance policy is required first).
- **Q:** how much extra to add windscreen cover?
  - **Expected:** Typically around 15% of the windscreen sum insured; claiming under this add-on does not affect your NCD and usually carries no excess.
- **Q:** car breakdown at night, is towing included in my policy?
  - **Expected:** Many comprehensive policies include or offer 24-hour roadside assistance: emergency towing to the nearest panel workshop, plus minor help like a jump-start, tyre change or fuel delivery.
- **Q:** what happens if i insure my car below its actual value?
  - **Expected:** Under-insurance can trigger the average clause, where claims are reduced proportionally — so always insure at the proper value.

## RHB Private Car Insurance (13 questions)

- **Q:** hi, what's the highest NCD discount I can get on my RHB car insurance?
  - *BM:* hi, berapa peratus NCD paling tinggi saya boleh dapat untuk insurans kereta RHB?
  - **Expected:** 55% — the NCD entitlement after 5 continuous claim-free years and beyond (25% after 1 year, 30% after 2, 38 1/3% after 3, 45% after 4).
- **Q:** my son is 19, if he drives my car and we claim, got extra excess or not?
  - **Expected:** Yes — a Compulsory Excess of RM400 applies (on top of any excess in the Schedule) if the driver is under 21 years old, holds a Provisional (P) or Learner (L) licence, or is not a named driver.
- **Q:** if I damage someone else's property in an accident, how much does the policy cover?
  - *BM:* kalau saya rosakkan harta benda orang lain dalam kemalangan, berapa had perlindungan polisi?
  - **Expected:** Up to a maximum of RM3 million for third party property damage (death or bodily injury to third parties is covered for an unlimited amount).
- **Q:** can I increase the third party property damage limit above 3 million?
  - **Expected:** Yes — for an additional premium, Endorsement 105 can extend the third party property damage limit up to RM20 million (e.g. up to RM4 million costs 15% of Third Party Premium, up to RM20 million costs 60%).
- **Q:** just had an accident, what's the 24 hour accident assist hotline number?
  - **Expected:** Call the Accident Assist Call Centre (AACC) 24-hours nationwide helpline at 1-300-22-1188 or 15-500 for immediate road assistance or tow service.
- **Q:** after an accident how long do I have to make the police report?
  - *BM:* lepas kemalangan, berapa lama saya ada untuk buat report polis?
  - **Expected:** You must report all accidents to the police within 24 hours, as required by law.
- **Q:** how many days do I have to send back the completed claim form?
  - **Expected:** Within twenty-one (21) days from the date of your notification of the incident (and you must notify the insurer within 7 days, or within 30 days if you are physically disabled and hospitalised).
- **Q:** is flood damage covered under this policy?
  - *BM:* polisi ni cover kerosakan banjir tak?
  - **Expected:** No — flood, storm, typhoon and other convulsions of nature are excluded, unless you pay additional premium for the Inclusion of Special Perils cover (Endorsement 57).
- **Q:** driving into Singapore next week, do I need to add anything to my policy?
  - **Expected:** Yes — Singapore law requires cover for Legal Liability to Passengers (LLP), which is not in the basic policy, so you need to purchase Endorsement 100.
- **Q:** if I cancel my policy within the first month, how much premium refund do I get?
  - **Expected:** 75% of the total premium (short-period rate for a period of insurance not exceeding 1 month), provided no claim was incurred before cancellation.
- **Q:** my car is 10 years old, if they repair with new original parts how much betterment do I have to pay?
  - **Expected:** 40% — the rate of betterment for cars aged 10 years and above (scale: under 5 years 0%, 5 years 15%, rising to 40% at 10 years and above).
- **Q:** how many panel workshops does RHB insurance have?
  - **Expected:** More than 700 panel workshops nationwide.
- **Q:** if I get charged for dangerous driving after an accident, will the insurance pay my legal fees?
  - **Expected:** Yes — legal costs are paid up to a maximum of RM2,000 to defend you or your authorised driver, provided the costs are incurred with the insurer's prior written agreement (penalties imposed are not paid).

## RHB Motorist Personal Accident (10 questions)

- **Q:** Hi, what's the age limit to be covered under the RHB motorist personal accident plan?
  - *BM:* Hi, had umur berapa untuk dilindungi bawah plan kemalangan diri pemandu RHB ni?
  - **Expected:** Covers drivers and/or passengers aged from 1 year old to 70 years old (both ages inclusive).
- **Q:** How much is the accidental death payout for the driver under Plan D?
  - **Expected:** RM240,000 accidental death benefit for the driver under Plan D (passenger gets RM48,000).
- **Q:** For the medical expenses reimbursement, how long after the accident can I still claim the treatment costs?
  - **Expected:** Medical expenses must be incurred within 52 weeks from the date of accident; it pays the actual cost per person for medical and surgical treatment, trained nurses and hospitalisation.
- **Q:** My car is a 5 seater, how much is the yearly premium for Plan A?
  - *BM:* Kereta saya 5 seater, berapa premium setahun untuk Plan A?
  - **Expected:** RM50 annual premium for Plan A with seating capacity 1-5 (subject to 6% service tax and RM10 stamp duty).
- **Q:** Does this motorist PA cover me if I drive to Singapore?
  - *BM:* Plan motorist PA ni cover tak kalau saya bawa kereta ke Singapore?
  - **Expected:** Yes - the plan provides coverage for personal accidents in Malaysia, Singapore and Brunei only.
- **Q:** What's the 24 hour hotline for RHB Auto Assist emergency motor assistance?
  - *BM:* Nombor hotline 24 jam RHB Auto Assist untuk bantuan kecemasan motor berapa ya?
  - **Expected:** 1300 880 881, available 24 hours daily.
- **Q:** How much stamp duty do I need to pay for this policy?
  - **Expected:** RM10 stamp duty is charged (premiums are also subject to 6% service tax).
- **Q:** If I cancel my motorist PA policy myself, do I get any refund?
  - **Expected:** No - if the Insured requests cancellation, there is no refund of premium. (Only if the Company cancels with 14 days written notice, and no claims were made, does the Insured get a pro-rata refund.)
- **Q:** After my application is approved, how long until I receive my policy schedule?
  - **Expected:** The Policy Schedule will be delivered within 30 working days from the date the application is approved.
- **Q:** Will the PA still pay out if the accident happened because the driver was drunk?
  - **Expected:** No - the policy excludes accidents arising out of the effect or influence of alcohol or drugs not prescribed by a qualified medical practitioner.
