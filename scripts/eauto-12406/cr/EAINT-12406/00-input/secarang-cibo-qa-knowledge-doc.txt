
# Secarang & CIBO QA Knowledge
 · 
Secarang is the public (B2C) car and motorcycle insurance site. It has no user login, pays through the Fiuu gateway, and records every transaction in CIBO. CIBO is the separate back office that records Secarang and eAuto insurance transactions. This doc collects what the eAuto QA knowledge base knows about both, with a source for each fact. Items marked [unconfirmed] are leads, not facts. Logins, passwords and real IC numbers are left out on purpose.
## 1. What Secarang is
Secarang is a separate public insurance site, not an eAuto portal. A member of the public types a plate number, an IC or SSM number and a postcode, picks an insurer, pays, and gets a receipt.
- No user login. The only gate on staging is a site password. [source: secarang-insurance automation, sessionFixture.ts]
- Three systems, shared data. eAuto, Secarang and CIBO are separate systems. CIBO is the back office that holds transactions from both eAuto and Secarang. [QA team, 2026-08-13; SRD EAINT-12151 §2.1.1 assumption 1: "CIBO covers both eAuto and Secarang insurance transactions"]
- Every Secarang test is a two-system test: Secarang for the purchase, CIBO for the record. Secarang has no transaction listing of its own [unconfirmed].
- Built as an Angular single-page app. Fields use formcontrolname, components use app-* tags. Screen URLs after /car-insurance are not recorded.
- Shares insurers with eAuto, and all insurers share one payment gateway. So one insurer is enough for a payment-channel test. [QA team via Teams, 2026-09-11]

## 2. Environments and access
Secarang staging runs at https://staging.secarang.com/<site>; the two sites in use are /ia and /preprod. Ask the dev which site holds a CR before the first run.
- Entry link: https://staging.secarang.com/ia/car-insurance (car) and <base>/motorcycle-insurance (motorcycle). Every later screen is reached by clicking.
- Site password: a password-only page, no username. The same password works on /ia and /preprod. [QA team, 2026-10-19] Ask the QA team for it; it is not in this doc.
- The gate can come back on the Fiuu popup and again on the success page.
- Host per CR: EAINT-12406 (Allianz) is tested on staging/ia. [QA team, 2026-10-27] The EAINT-8608 Zurich branch was on /preprod.
- /ia vs /preprod data: /ia quotes made-up plates (e.g. HXA0001A–HXA0020A); /preprod refuses them with "Sorry, we're unable to retrieve your vehicle information." [live run, 2026-10-19]
- Payment mode per site: mock payment was enabled on /ia only on 2026-08-14 (Fiuu sandbox IP issue); /preprod needs the dev to enable it. On 2026-05-20 /ia was linked to production BoldPay; the QA lead said not to pay for Macrokiosk insurance transactions then. Check before paying. [Teams, 2026-05-20 and 2026-08-14]
- Backend hosts: secarang-backend at staging/ins, CIBO frontend at staging/cibo (https://staging.secarang.com/cibo).
- Production regression: quote only, then stop at the payment page (Step 4 or 5). QA cannot make a paid Secarang transaction in production. [Deployment Checklist, 20261020 AM R43]
## 3. Purchase flow
The purchase runs in six steps after the home form: Car Details → All Quotations → Add-ons → Confirmation → Payment → Success. [Miro board EAINT-8608; secarang-insurance automation]
- Home form. Tab Car or Motorcycle, then Private or Company. Three fields: Vehicle Plate No., Owner IC / Company SSM, Postal Code. Click [Get quotation]; a "Bear with us" loader shows.
- The three inputs have no id or name; automation finds them by position.
- Car and motorcycle plates can go through either tab. [BA via Miro EAINT-8608]
- Step 1 — Car Details ("Are these your vehicle details?"). Not always shown. Shows Plate, Make, Model, Year, Seat, CC, Variant, Transmission, Current Coverage, NCD. Tick the declarations; pick a variant if a dropdown shows (most cars have none). [Get quotation] starts disabled while details validate.
- Step 2 — All Quotations. One card per insurer. A card can be available, or say "Quotation unavailable". Cards can take 10–20 s, sometimes a minute.
- Sum insured dropdown on a card changes the price.
- The postcode can be edited here with the [Edit] button.
- Step 3 — Add-ons. Add-on cards with ADD / REMOVE. Zurich pre-ticks "All Drivers" with a popup. For motorbikes, "All Riders" is mandatory. A reminder popup may show after Continue.
- Step 4 — Confirmation ("Confirm and Pay"). Owner name, email, phone, address 1–3, optional discount code. Changing gender must re-price. The page shows IC with dashes and phone with +60.
- Step 5 — Payment ("How would you like to pay?"). Pick a channel; Fiuu opens in a popup window. See section 5.
- Step 6 — Success. Main window shows "Thank you for your purchase!" with Receipt No, Purchase Date, Payment Method, and a [Receipt] PDF download. A WhatsApp and an email receipt follow.
Branches to test:
- Error after the home form: popup "Sorry, we're unable to retrieve your vehicle information." (e.g. fake company SSM).
- No insurer quotes: popup "Ops, there is no instant quotation available from our insurers for your car at the moment."
- "Kindly confirm your navigation" popup ("By leaving this page, you would need to restart your purchasing process") on the car-details page. Not a vehicle answer.
- Payment failed → back on payment page with "Unsuccessful payment You may try again."; no receipt.
- Payment cancelled → back on payment page, quotation kept. [SRD EAINT-12107 REQ-008]
- Company owner: "there are no insurance in secarang that supports company" [QA team, 2026-10-20]. Allianz quotes only individual owners on renewal. [SRD EAINT-12406 REQ-002]
- Lonpac 14-day grace: "Policy expired and is still within 14-day renewal period | Able to purchase Lonpac insurance"; after 14 days, not possible. [TS EAINT-9062 rows 2-3]
- Discount line on receipt / email / WhatsApp shows only for codes MF / EAUTO / ER; hidden for other codes and when no code. [TS EAINT-8331/8334/7199 rows 14, 17]

Main window stays on "How would you like to pay?" with the same vehicle and premium. Before [OK] the main window looks the same, so being back on the payment page alone does not prove a cancel. [live 12107_TS10, 2026-10-20]
- After ShopeePay [Pay] the simulator tab lands on an /ia/auth/login?returnUrl=/payment/check/<receipt> page; ignore it. The result shows in the main window.
- Real-device legs the sandbox cannot replace: ShopeePay completes in the Shopee app (REQ-009); GrabPay asks the customer to approve the login in the Grab app (REQ-010).
- Sandbox money, real record: a sandbox payment still creates a real Secarang record on that host, and it shows in CIBO (Paid / Failed).
- No amount limit on the e-wallet rails (REQ-004); BNPL tenure is Shopee's / Grab's business, not stored by Secarang (§2.1.1 assumption 3).
## 6. CIBO — where Secarang transactions are recorded
CIBO is the only place a Secarang purchase can be checked after payment. Every Secarang payment test needs a CIBO check. Staging CIBO for Secarang is https://staging.secarang.com/cibo.
- Modules that hold Secarang rows: Transaction Management (Transaction Listing → Transaction Details), Payment Management, Payment Report, Commission Report, CRM. [Miro EAINT-8608; flow-cibo notes]
- Platform filter / badge: Secarang or eAuto; Macrokiosk renewals show Secarang-MK. [Jira EAINT-8669, EAINT-12218]
- UCD columns show a literal "-" on Secarang rows (UCD, UCD Name, Discount Code, Road Tax, Remark). This is a rule, not missing data. Any CR adding a UCD column needs a Secarang row to prove the "-" case. [BA testing plan, EAINT-12058, 2026-08-13]
- Payment Mode history: "RMS" = Secarang before the Fiuu cutoff; "Fiuu" = Secarang after it; "Bold Pay" = Macrokiosk transactions; "RHB" = eAuto. So BoldPay is the Macrokiosk gateway, not a Secarang one. [SharePoint TS EAINT-11541 rows 3-10]
- Payment Mode format after EAINT-12327: <gateway> | <method> – <details>. ShopeePay Full Amount and SPayLater both show as Fiuu | eWallet – ShopeePay; the "ShopeePay - SPayLater" filter was dropped. [Teams, EAINT-12107 CR chat, 2026-10-20]
- FPX format (SA, 2026-10-20): "Fiuu | Online Banking – FPX-Bank Name". Staging still showed "Fiuu" alone → raised as QA-Issue EAINT-12566 (On Hold).
- "EW" in Payment Reference ID applies to new data only. [2026-10-20]
- Payment Report lists insured transactions only, and is slow: about 2 minutes per reference search.
- Commission: Secarang commission follows Discount Code Management; eAuto gets 10% automatically. [SharePoint TS #140 row 10]
- Agency in reports: Allianz sales appear "under agency Serba Sentral in CIBO payment and commission reports". [SRD EAINT-12406 v1.1 §2.1.3 b]
- A Secarang transaction taken to Step 4 or 5 creates a CIBO record even without payment. [Deployment Checklist, 20250630 R53]
## 7. CIBO overview
CIBO is its own portal, not part of eAuto and not part of Secarang. It has its own Jira component (CIBO) in EAINT. [QA team, 2026-08-13]
- One listing, two sources. The Transaction Listing holds eAuto and Secarang insurance transactions in one table.
- Third place to verify eAuto insurance. An eAuto insurance transaction is checked in the UCD portal, the BO portal, and CIBO. The EAINT-11864 TS says: "Cross check the details in the relevant pages: a. UCD Insurance Transaction and Details page · b. BO Insurance Transaction and Details page · c. CIBO". A change in eAuto can surface as a CIBO defect, and the other way round.
- Environment is per ticket. Examples: EAINT-12058 used eAuto at staging/uat4 with Secarang at staging/cibo; EAINT-12151 used cibo/staging. Read the environment from the ticket. [QA team, 2026-08-14]
- Login form: Login ID + password; the Login button stays disabled until both are filled. Ask the QA team for a staging admin login.
- Sidebar routes: /cibo/dashboard, user-account-management, crm, transaction-management, payment-management, referred-risk-log, payment-report, commission-report, visitor-management, agent-management, discount-management, macrokiosk-template-management. [live staging, 2026-10-16]

## 8. CIBO pages
Three pages carry most CR testing: Transaction Management, Payment Management and Payment Report. "Transaction Management" is the page; "Transaction Listing" is the table on it; "Transaction Management Listing" names the same table. [SRD EAINT-12327 §2.2.3; SRD EAINT-12151 §2.2.1]
### Transaction Management
- Filters: Platform, Date Range Type, date range, Insurance Company, Transaction Status, Submission to JPJ Status, Road Tax, Discount Code, free-text Search. [Search] and "Clear all". 20 rows per page.
- Example filter values: Platform Secarang / eAuto; Insurance Company Pemaju Ekslusif (an agency label); Transaction Status Insurance Created; JPJ Accepted; Road Tax Exempted; Discount Code Included. [TS EAINT-12151 TS04/TS05]
- Listing columns, in order: # · Transaction Ref. No. · Transaction Created Date · Insurance Created Date · UCD · UCD Name · Vehicle No · Vehicle Type · Insured · Payment · Payment Mode · Discount Code · Road Tax · Ins. Co. · Transaction Status · JPJ · Remark. [EAINT-12151 mockup; SRD EAINT-12327 REQ-003]
- Cell shapes: Ref No. with a platform badge; Insured = name over IC; Payment = amount, Paid/Pending badge, then DD-MM-YYYY HH:mm; Ins. Co. = insurer over agency badge.
- Print and Export buttons. Transaction Details opens per row.
### Transaction Management export (CSV)
- File name Transaction_Management_<yyyyMMddHHmm>.csv, UTF-8 BOM. Line 1 = title, line 2 blank, line 3 = header. [SharePoint TS #140 EAINT-9979 row 2; live staging, 2026-10-16]
- 40 columns, including Platform, Company Name, Company Registration No., UCD Name, Insured Name / ID, Gross Premium, Service Tax, Stamp Duty, Total Premium, Discount, Payment Amount, Insurance Commission Amount, Payment Status / At / Month / Year, Insurance Company, Agency, E-Certificate No. / Policy No., Coverage Type, JPJ, Remark, Special Remark.
- Column order differs from the screen: in the CSV, Payment Mode sits between Discount Code and Payment Amount (RM). [live staging, 2026-10-16]
- Export is a second read path. A new listing column must be checked on screen and in the export. [QA team, 2026-08-13]
### Payment Management
- Payment Channel filter: a checkbox tree. Group "Fiuu - eWallet" holds GrabPay, GrabPay - PayLater by Grab, ShopeePay (no SPayLater option; correct per BA, 2026-10-20).
- Ticking the group ticks all options. Unticking one option clears the group tick; clicking the group again re-ticks all. [live, 2026-10-16]
- Listing shows Payment Reference ID, Payment Channel (e.g. Fiuu eWallet GrabPay). Export names the column Payment Reference ID (page: Payment Ref ID) and writes Fiuu eWallet | GrabPay.
- An empty listing downloads no file on Export.
### Payment Report
- Lists insured transactions only. Carries UCD Name (since EAINT-11934, live 2026-07-24), User ID, Payment Mode, Payment Ref ID.
- Always narrow by "Insurance Created From / Till" to one week. Unfiltered or one-month searches take about 2 minutes per reference. [QA team, 2026-10-19]
- The date fields are read-only date pickers; pick dates in the calendar. The two calendars limit each other, so when moving the window later set "Till" first.
### Referred Risk Log and Macrokiosk Template Management
- Referred Risk Log has an export. Macrokiosk Template Management lists the WhatsApp reminder templates (see section 11). [Jira EAINT-12218]
## 9. CIBO business rules
### Money columns and discount codes
[SharePoint TS #140 EAINT-9979 rows 4-10; TS #128 EAINT-10052 rows 10-13]
- "Gross Premium (RM) refers to total premium amount after NCD (No Claim Discount) plus any add-ons."
- "Service Tax = Gross Premium (RM) x 8%"; stamp duty is always RM10.
- Total Premium = total payable before discount.
- Discount codes: "EAUTOOFF10 - Discount 10% of Gross Premium (automatically applied to eAuto transaction) 2. RJC7 - Discount 7% of Gross Premium 3. COTEAM - Discount 5% of Gross Premium 4. SCW5 - Discount 5% of Gross Premium". No code → " 0.00 ".
- Insurance commission is "10% applied automatically to transaction made in eAuto"; Secarang commission follows Discount Code Management. Commission to sub-agent: RJC7 3%.
- "Total Direct Cost = Discount to Customers + Commission to Sub Agent"; "Gross Profit = Insurance Commission Amount − Total Direct Cost".
### Dates (EAINT-12151, deployed 2026-09-30)
- New Date Range Type filter: Transaction Creation Date (renamed) or Insurance Creation Date (new). [SRD REQ-001/002]
- Listing: "Created Date" → Transaction Created Date; new Insurance Created Date next to it, DD-MM-YYYY HH:mm when approved, - otherwise. [REQ-003/004/005]
- Insurance Created Date = payment date-time. SRD change log: "Clarified Insurance Creation Date refers to Payment Date Time". [SRD v1.0 §1.3]
- The Insurance Creation Date filter excludes rows with no such date. [REQ-006]
- Export: "Created At" → Transaction Created At, new Insurance Created At right after it; Print carries the new column. [REQ-007/008/009]
### UCD columns (EAINT-11934, EAINT-12058)
- UCD Name sits between UCD and Vehicle No, on the listing and in the export. Secarang rows show -.
- Records before 2025 show no UCD Name because of an earlier data migration. Known and accepted, not a defect.
- EAINT-11934 delivered UCD Name on the Payment Report only (live 2026-07-24). The Transaction Listing half was cut and moved to EAINT-12058.
- EAINT-12058 adds Full Name (created / paid / last update) and Login ID (paid). Only Full Name shows in CIBO; the rest are checked through dev DB screenshots. [dev, 2026-08-13]
- An insurance row has only two actor events, create and pay. So "last update" always equals the payer; expect at most two different names.

## 10. CIBO testing tips and traps
- Wait for the first load before searching. Opening a listing starts a slow unfiltered load (Payment Management: 222 pages). If you click [Search] before it finishes, the unfiltered answer can land last and replace your filtered rows. The export still uses the filter, so page and file disagree. [live probe, 2026-10-20]
- Export a narrow range. A Transaction Management export with no filters (~7,900 rows) gave no file after 3+ minutes. [live, 2026-10-16]
- Payment Report: one-week window (section 8). Each reference search otherwise takes about 2 minutes.
- Verify in all three places for eAuto insurance: UCD, BO, CIBO. CIBO is a separate read path and can disagree.
- New column = backfill question. Decide up front whether old rows should fill or stay blank, and get it stated.
- Live join or snapshot? Rename a user after a transaction and re-read the listing to prove whether a name is captured at transaction time or joined live. [unconfirmed which]
- Dev data patching is accepted to create rows the sandbox cannot make (e.g. ShopeePay, PayLater by Grab). Patched rows prove display rules only. A patch may change Payment Mode but not Payment Channel (BR60006259, 2026-10-20). [Jira EAINT-12107, 2026-09-09]
- Payment cell text has no space before the badge (RM884.48Paid 07-10-2026 10:20), so a word-boundary match on "Paid" fails.
- Sidebar collapses to icons after a wide listing; menu text is then hidden.
- Oldest rows are eAuto from 2022 (Payment Mode RHB, or - when unpaid). Oldest Secarang rows show RMS.
- Filters are ng-select dropdowns; date inputs are read-only date pickers. Automation must click, not type.
- Automation exists for EAINT-12107/12327 CIBO checks (12107_TS06, 12327_TS10–TS18). It can find a matching reference on its own by reading the newest and oldest pages of Transaction Management.
## 11. Macrokiosk WhatsApp renewal reminders
A cron job sends WhatsApp renewal reminders through Macrokiosk at 30, 14 and 7 days before policy expiry. The customer taps [Pay Now] and lands on Secarang Step 4 to pay. [Miro eAuto - Macrokiosk; Jira EAINT-12218]
- Three fixed sources: "Customer records are drawn from 3 sources: eSTM with insurance, eSTM without insurance, and Secarang." [SRD EAINT-12218 v1.1 REQ-003]
- Nine templates: "9 Macrokiosk renewal reminder templates are used in total, grouped into 3 sets of 3. Each template is used by 1 source at 1 reminder point." [REQ-001]
- Exact-day pickup: "A customer is included in a run only where the policy expiry date falls exactly on the reminder point for that run." [REQ-003]
- One source per customer: "no customer is sent more than 1 message on the same date." [§2.1.1 assumption 4] A record with no policy expiry date never gets a reminder.
- No more reminders after payment: "Transaction Payment = Failed / OK on 30 days, wont pickup on 14 / 7 days". Opt-out numbers are skipped; no message when there is no quote. [Miro, 2026-10-28]
- No WhatsApp when the cron cannot quote: vehicle details not matched, all insurers hit refer-risk, or all insurer APIs down → "Should NOT trigger MKWA API" (SECPN_TS16, TS18, TS20). The same set exists for eAuto insurance (eAIPN_) and eSTM (eSTMPN_) records.
- eSIM levers for cron negatives: "XG for vehicle details not found / - MD for TokioMarine refer risk / - XF for Lonpac refer risk / - XH for both refer risk". [Miro]
- Trigger limit: "MacroKiosk Trigger Limit: Max. 3 times" (what it counts is not stated).
- Payment link expires 24 hours after the WhatsApp message; the message ends "Reply OPT-OUT to stop updates." [Miro Macro Kiosk - Workflow; Jira EAINT-10444]
- The cron cannot be triggered on demand. On staging it is set to run every 30 minutes from the hour. Each staging run can stand for the next day's run.

## 13. Secarang traps and test-data tips
- Test data on /ia: made-up plates HXA0001A–HXA0020A and ZZZ1A with a real IC and postcode 55000 quote Lonpac (e.g. HONDA HR-V, RM884.48). Made-up plate QAT9100A gets "no instant quotation". A fake company SSM gets "unable to retrieve your vehicle information". [live runs, 2026-10-19/20] Ask the QA team for the IC used.
- Phone field keeps "+60". Typing "0189812839" gives "+600189812839" and the error "At least 12 digits including +60". Type the number without the leading 0.
- Responsive duplicates. The page renders a hidden second copy of the bank grid and the quotation cards. Only act on visible elements.
- Positional home-form inputs (no ids). A layout change can fill the wrong field; read each value back.
- [Get quotation] starts disabled on Car Details; clicking early does nothing.
- Add-ons [Continue] can be clicked before the page loads, leaving you on Add-ons. Wait for the page.
- The Fiuu popup closes itself after Pay. Register for the popup before clicking the payment tile.
- The site gate reappears on the Fiuu popup and on the success page. The Fiuu Bank Simulator has its own username + password; do not type the site password there.
- No "stop before payment" switch in the Secarang automation. A dry run must stop at the quote.
- Takaful Malaysia is often not quotable on staging; find a vehicle that quotes it before test day.
- eSIM may not show TPFT for Takaful; use live API data for TPFT cases [unconfirmed for Zurich].
- Do not set a VN prefix for insurance scenarios by default; different prefixes drive different eSIM outcomes. [QA team, 2026-10-27]
- Allianz test data is unknown. Check which plates Allianz quotes before buying.
- Do not raise ShopeePay as broken on old staging notes; it works end to end on /ia since 2026-10-20.
- Zurich card wording must name Pemaju Eksklusif, not reuse the Takaful Malaysia distributor text (same mix-up as bug EAINT-10971).
